The Battle of the Bulge: Hitler's Final Western Offensive

In the bitter winter of December 1944, German forces launched a desperate, surprise counteroffensive through the heavily forested Ardennes region, aiming to split Allied lines and capture the vital port of Antwerp.

The Bulge and Bastogne

Taking American units completely by surprise in freezing weather conditions, the German panzers drove a deep "bulge" into Allied lines. However, stubborn defense by encircled troops at Bastogne and rapid armored counterattacks led by Patton's Third Army shattered the offensive, exhausting Germany's remaining reserves.
