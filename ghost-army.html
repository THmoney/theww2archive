The Ghost Army: Deception, Inflatables, and Tactical Illusion

The 23rd Headquarters Special Troops was a top-secret tactical deception unit comprising artists, architects, sound engineers, and camouflage experts recruited from elite art schools across the United States.

Inflatable Armor and Sonic Deception

Armed with inflatable rubber tanks, dummy aircraft, and massive multi-watt speakers broadcasting recorded sound effects of truck convoys, they misled German reconnaissance forces regarding the positioning and strength of Allied divisions across Western Europe.
