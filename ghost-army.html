The Ghost Army: Deception, Inflatables, and Illusion

Published on November 3, 2026

The 23rd Headquarters Special Troops was a tactical deception unit comprising artists, sound engineers, and camouflage experts. Armed with inflatable rubber tanks, dummy aircraft, and massive audio speakers broadcasting truck convoys miles away, they successfully fooled German intelligence about Allied troop locations.
